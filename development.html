01 — The idea

Strategy should be the hard part.
Not the interface.

Classic RTS asks you to be a commander and then scores you on typing speed. Greenmantle moves the difficulty back where it belongs: choosing the right plan, and the right ground to fight on.

⚔

Intent over execution

Assign a mission and a doctrine once. Squads carry it out, adapt to what they meet, and fall back when you told them to. No re-issuing the same order.

⛰

Terrain decides fights

High ground, flanking and facing all multiply damage directly. A smaller force on better ground beats a bigger one on worse ground — every time.

⚙

No deathballs

Pack more than twenty fighters into one place and they get measurably worse. A 35-strong blob barely edges out a disciplined 22. Spread out or lose.

⛪

Set-and-forget economy

Point a base at a resource once and every worker it builds goes there and starts working. Worker babysitting is a bug, not a skill.

02 — The world

Not civilizations.
Forces of nature.

Four powers, each an element at war with the others. Same problems — gather, expand, fight — answered in ways that feel genuinely different rather than re-skinned.

Cohort Death

A war-machine that outlived its builders. Fossil-pale stone, gently overgrown, still running on a schedule nobody remembers setting.

Mycora Life

Hostile terrain given intent. Spreads rather than marches, and takes ground by growing over it. Cheap to plant, cheap to burn back.

Conclave Water

Coordination that literally flows. Canals, locks and reflecting pools carry its network across the map, following the rivers.

Titanfolk Earth

Landscape that stood up. Slow because it is genuinely that size, and almost impossible to move once it decides where to stand.

06 — Design language

The target is not realism.
It is believable myth.

Warm painterly light, natural asymmetry, and weathering treated as a real material rather than a decal. Ancient machinery, gently overgrown.

Everything below is concept art, not in-game footage. These are explorations that establish silhouette, material and faction identity. The game currently renders stylized placeholder geometry — none of this has been modelled yet.
